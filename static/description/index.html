AIRIV Indonesia UMKM Tax Ledger

PP 55/2022 Final PPh 0.5% and Omzet Ledger for Odoo

A practical Indonesian UMKM compliance workspace for monthly gross turnover, Rp 500 million threshold tracking, final income tax calculation, billing evidence, and executive dashboard review.

Odoo 18 Community PP 55/2022 PPh Final 0.5% Rp 500M Threshold AIRIV

Core Capabilities

AIRIV UMKM Indonesia converts manual omzet bookkeeping into a controlled Odoo workflow with tax logic, payment evidence, and dashboard clarity.

Ledger

Monthly omzet records

Record fiscal year, month, taxpayer type, company, gross turnover, e-Billing code, NTPN, payment date, and status.

Compliance

PP 55/2022 calculation

Compute cumulative prior turnover, non-taxable portion, taxable DPP, and PPh Final 0.5% based on taxpayer type.

Dashboard

Executive tax visibility

Review year-to-date turnover, threshold utilization, taxable base, and total final tax obligation in one workspace.

Feature & Workflow Automation

1

Record turnover

Enter monthly gross turnover for the selected fiscal period and taxpayer category.

2

Calculate tax

Let Odoo compute prior cumulative turnover, non-taxable portion, taxable DPP, and PPh Final 0.5%.

3

Track billing

Move the record to billed status when e-Billing information is ready.

4

Confirm payment

Store NTPN and payment date to close the monthly tax evidence loop.

Installation Guidance

Install

Add to Odoo addons

Clone branch 18.0, add the module folder to your addons path, restart Odoo, update the apps list, and install the module.

Configure

Set taxpayer context

Confirm company currency and use the correct taxpayer type before entering monthly turnover records.

Operate

Close each tax period

Calculate the month, input e-Billing evidence, then confirm NTPN and payment date after payment.

Configuration Checklist

CompanyConfirm company and currency before entering tax records.
Taxpayer typeChoose individual taxpayer or business entity consistently for the fiscal year.
Monthly dataEnter gross turnover in chronological order for cleaner cumulative calculation review.
Billing evidenceStore e-Billing code, NTPN, and payment date after official tax payment steps.
Dashboard reviewOpen the dashboard to check year-to-date turnover, taxable base, tax obligation, and threshold utilization.
